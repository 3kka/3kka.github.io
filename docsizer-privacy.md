## Privacy Policy

Applic Labs ("we," "us," or "our") provides the **Docsizer** application (the "App") to help users resize and convert document formats. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of your information when you use Docsizer.

## 1. Local Document Processing

Docsizer is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. All document resizing, formatting, and conversion processes occur entirely locally on your device.

* **No Server Uploads:** Your documents, files, and images are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers or any third-party cloud infrastructure.
* **Full Offline Functionality:** Docsizer is fully functional without an internet connection, guaranteeing that your files remain exclusively on your local hardware at all times.

## 2. Information Collection and Use

We do not automatically collect, track, or harvest any personal data, telemetry, or usage metrics while you operate Docsizer.

You are provided with an option to set up a user profile within the App, which may include your:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Gender
* Date of Birth

**This profile is completely optional and is not mandatory.** You can access and utilize all of Docsizer’s core document manipulation features without providing any of this information. If you choose to input this data, it is used strictly for in-app personalization and is stored locally on your device.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because your documents are processed locally and we do not collect telemetry or require personal data, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data or files with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.

## 4. Security

We value your trust. Any optional profile information you choose to provide is stored securely on your device's local storage. We recommend utilizing your device's built-in security features (such as PIN, biometrics, or password protection) to further secure your local device data.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Docsizer does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and publishing the revised version within the App or on our official platforms.
